I am currently a postdoctoral fellow in Biostatistics at [**Emory University**](https://www.emory.edu/) in the [**Department of Biostatistics and Bioinformatics**](https://sph.emory.edu/departments/biostatistics-bioinformatics) at the Rollins School of Public Health. Working with [**Emily N. Peterson**](https://sph.emory.edu/profile/faculty/emily-peterson), I develop Bayesian hierarchical spatio-temporal and measurement error methods for small-area mortality estimation, with a focus on correcting racial misclassification and population undercounting in American Indian and Alaska Native health data. I also collaborate with [**Paul A. Parker**](https://www.paparkerstat.com/) and [**Robert B. Lund**](https://engineering.ucsc.edu/voices/robert-lund-faculty/) on spatial statistical methods and their applications to climate science.
